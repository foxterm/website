Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About FoxTerm

Security, Pro licensing, and native performance — explained plainly.

Q: What is FoxTerm and what core features does it offer?

FoxTerm is an all-in-one, privacy-first server management application built natively for macOS and iOS. It unifies high-performance SSH terminal access, SFTP file management, Metal-accelerated VNC remote desktop, real-time system telemetry, and visual Docker & Podman container control.

Q: What is the difference between the free version and Pro? How does the Pro subscription work across devices?

The free version is not a crippled demo — every capability, from the SSH terminal and SFTP to Metal-accelerated VNC, real-time telemetry, and Docker & Podman management, is fully unlocked. The only limit is the number of nodes (servers) you can manage. Subscribing to FoxTerm Pro removes that cap for unlimited nodes, and a single subscription simultaneously unlocks both macOS and iOS/iPadOS clients under the same Apple ID, with encrypted configuration sync via your personal iCloud container.

Q: How does FoxTerm secure my server keys and connection credentials?

FoxTerm operates under a strict zero-telemetry policy with 100% localized data encryption. Server credentials and SSH private keys are hardware-isolated inside Apple Secure Enclave and Keychain, with no data ever sent to third-party servers.

Q: How is FoxTerm different from Electron-based terminal clients?

FoxTerm is not an Electron wrapper. It is engineered from the ground up using Native Swift, Metal hardware acceleration, and C-native libraries (OpenSSL, libssh, libvncclient), delivering near-instant startup, ultra-low resource usage, and native iOS optimization.

Q: Which platforms and operating systems are supported?

FoxTerm is built natively for Apple platforms: it runs on macOS 14.0 or later and iOS/iPadOS 17.0 or later, including iPhone and iPad, with a single Universal Purchase unlocking every device. There is no Windows, Android, or Linux desktop build — FoxTerm is engineered exclusively for the native Apple stack.

Q: Which protocols and authentication methods are supported?

FoxTerm supports SSH, SFTP, and VNC connections, and manages Docker and Podman engines over SSH. It ships with modern ciphers and key types such as Ed25519, split-pane terminal sessions, command snippets, drag-and-drop SFTP transfers, and visual management of containers, images, and volumes.

Q: Does FoxTerm collect data or route my connections through its servers?

No. FoxTerm connects directly from your device to your own servers and never proxies your traffic. It enforces a strict zero-telemetry policy — no analytics, no activity tracking, and no connection logs. Credentials and private keys are encrypted on-device and isolated inside Apple Keychain and Secure Enclave.

Q: How do I get Pro on macOS, and how does iOS activation work?

There are two ways to get Pro on macOS. The Mac App Store edition is a monthly or yearly subscription billed by Apple — manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings, and renewals and refunds are handled by Apple. The notarized edition is a direct, signed macOS build that you unlock with an activation code purchased at pay.foxterm.app. Whichever Mac edition you choose, the iOS app is activated automatically; the iOS app itself is free on the App Store.
