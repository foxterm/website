Welcome to FoxTerm, your all-in-one server management tool with seamless support for macOS and iOS. At FoxTerm, we treat your privacy and data security with the utmost seriousness. This Privacy Policy is designed to articulate our unwavering commitment: ensuring that all your information remains exclusively and absolutely within your control. We believe the core value of a professional management tool lies in security and trust, not data collection.

1. 核心原则：零数据收集 1. Core Principle: Zero Data Collection

我们庄严承诺：我们不会收集您的任何个人数据或信息。 We solemnly promise: We do not collect any personal data or information from you.

FoxTerm 应用架构专为极高隐私和安全性设计。通过 FoxTerm 处理的所有服务器管理活动、配置详情、凭据及操作日志，均严格保存在您的本地设备上。这些数据绝不会离开您的设备，不会传输给我们，也不会共享给任何第三方。 The FoxTerm application is engineered for maximum privacy and high security. All server management activities, configuration details, credentials, and operation logs processed through the FoxTerm app are strictly stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us, nor shared with any third-party entities.

  • 本地存储：您的服务器信息、连接凭据、使用习惯及所有敏感数据仅保存在设备的本地文件系统中。 Localized Data Storage: Your server information, connection credentials, usage patterns, and all sensitive data reside solely within your local device's file system.
  • 无遥测与分析：我们未集成任何形式的数据分析工具、用户行为追踪器或遥测系统。我们无法得知您的任何应用使用情况。 No Telemetry or Analytics: We have not integrated any form of analytics tools, user behavior trackers, or telemetry systems. We do not know how you use the application.
  • 无日志与调试数据收集：即使发生应用错误或调试场景，也不会自动收集或发送诊断信息。所有日志生成均限制在本地，且需要您主动手动导出提供。 No Log or Debugging Data Collection: Even in the event of an application error or a debugging scenario, no diagnostic information is automatically collected or sent to us. Any potential log data generation is restricted to your local device and requires your active and manual action to export and provide to us.
  • IP 地址保护：您的 IP 地址、地理位置或任何网络标识符均不在我们的收集范围内。 IP Address Protection: Your IP address, geographical location, or any network identifiers are not within our scope of collection.

我们致力于确保本地设备上信息的绝对安全。应用采用严密的本地安全措施（如基于操作系统的本地数据加密机制），防止本地环境中的未授权访问。 We are committed to ensuring the absolute security of your information on your local device. While the data remains local, we employ robust security measures within the application (e.g., local data encryption mechanisms, depending on operating system capabilities) to protect against unauthorized access within your local environment.

您拥有完全控制权：您可以随时删除应用，所有相关数据将同时从本地清除，无需我们进行任何干预。 You have complete control: You can delete the application at any time, and all associated data will be removed simultaneously, without any intervention required from us.
